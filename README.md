# Theory of Isospatial Reflexive Dimensions (TIRD)

**A Unified Framework for Coexistent Mirror States Occupying a Single Spacetime Coordinate**

**Author:** Pangan, A. (2026)
**Status:** Manuscript submitted for publication and Nobel consideration.

### Abstract
Two people can stand in the same place, witness the same event, and still perceive, remember, and anticipate it differently. This manuscript proposes that such differences may amount to multiple distinguishable realities on a single Earth, that what one observer experiences as the present another may experience as the future, and that an observer's consciousness frequency may govern this difference.

This theory is formalised as six postulates. Consciousness frequency is defined as individual alpha frequency (Fi, Hz) measured from resting EEG. An observer's reality state is defined as the triple of accessible information, prior model, and predictive distribution (Dij).

### Theories Included
1. Theory of Multiple Realities on a Single Earth and Consciousness-Relative Time
2. Spatial and Temporal Momentum through Continuous Pi Movement
3. Sixth-Dimension Theory of Consciousness and Perceived Space
4. Theory of Isospatial Reflexive Dimensions (TIRD)
